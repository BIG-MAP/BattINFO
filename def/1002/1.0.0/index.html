Terms (7)

Dataset kinds concept scheme
https://w3id.org/battinfo/def/1002/DatasetKind
The kinds of dataset catalogued by Battery Data Commons. A dataset record carries one primary kind (data_group) and may carry further kinds (categories); all are expressed as dcat:theme on the dataset.
Durability data concept
https://w3id.org/battinfo/def/1002/DurabilityData
Datasets that follow cells through ageing: cycling or calendar-ageing campaigns, usually with periodic check-ups, so that the change of performance over life can be studied.
Field data concept
https://w3id.org/battinfo/def/1002/FieldData
Datasets recorded from cells, modules or packs in service outside the laboratory, such as vehicles and stationary storage, including drive-cycle and usage logs.
Modelling data concept
https://w3id.org/battinfo/def/1002/ModelingData
Datasets produced by or for models: parameter sets, simulation inputs and outputs, and the measurements used to fit or validate a model.
Performance data concept
https://w3id.org/battinfo/def/1002/PerformanceData
Datasets from laboratory tests that characterise how a cell performs at a given point in its life: capacity, internal resistance, impedance, open-circuit voltage, and their dependence on rate, temperature and state of charge.
Safety data concept
https://w3id.org/battinfo/def/1002/SafetyData
Datasets from abuse and safety testing: thermal, mechanical or electrical abuse, thermal runaway, and the heat, gas and venting behaviour observed.
Synthetic data concept
https://w3id.org/battinfo/def/1002/SyntheticData
Datasets generated by simulation or generative methods rather than measured on physical cells.
